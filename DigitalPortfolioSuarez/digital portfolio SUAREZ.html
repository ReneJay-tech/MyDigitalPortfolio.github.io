SEAITT MLMS: Learning Management System for Senior High School

Capstone Project

Click a screenshot to view it larger.

Description

A web-based LMS where Senior High School teachers can post lessons and exams and students can access materials and take assessments online. Built as a team of four using ASP.NET Core and Blazor with a MySQL database. My role: QA Tester. My work includes:

  • Testing SMS OTP verification: checking that codes arrive, expire on time, and that request limits block repeated sends
  • Testing anti-cheating exam features: trying actions like switching tabs during an exam to confirm they are detected and flagged
  • Checking performance: confirming frequently used pages load quickly and stay fast
  • Testing teacher and student flows: login, posting lessons and handouts, taking quizzes and viewing results
  • Reporting bugs with clear steps to reproduce, then re-testing fixes to make sure nothing else broke
